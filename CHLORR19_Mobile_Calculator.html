    How This Was Calculated

    Iron & Manganese corrections use documented oxidation stoichiometry (0.64 mg Cl₂/mg Fe, 1.29 mg Cl₂/mg Mn) and are added to the dose. pH, Temperature & Colour affect treatment efficiency but have no fixed CPHEEO/BIS dose-multiplier — they are shown as advisories, not applied as numeric multipliers, to avoid giving a falsely precise number.

    ⚠ Safety Note Base doses are CPHEEO/BIS-reference indicative baselines only. Actual dose MUST be verified by jar test before field application. NTU > 300 requires mandatory jar test — do not dose on this figure alone. This tool does not replace a qualified process engineer's sign-off.
